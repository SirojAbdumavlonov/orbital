# Code of conduct

Orbital follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and patient, assume good intent, criticise ideas rather than people, and keep discussions on topic. Harassment, insults and publishing other people's private information are not tolerated, in issues, pull requests or anywhere else the project is represented.

To report a problem, contact the maintainers privately through the email on their GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, close issues or block accounts that break these rules.
